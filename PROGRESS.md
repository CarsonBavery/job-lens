### Phase 2 readiness determination (2026-07-25)
**Verdict: ship it — merge Phase 2, move to Phase 3.** One real gap was found during review (tailored/AI-generated documents were unreachable after creation — no list, no link, nothing) and it was fixed and e2e-verified before this determination, not left as a caveat. What's *not* fixed and is explicitly being carried forward rather than blocking:
- **No rate limiting on Gemini calls.** A free-tier user can generate unlimited AI content today at real API cost with zero monetization gate. This isn't a Phase 2 bug so much as a pre-launch requirement — it belongs with Phase 4 (billing/tier enforcement) or Phase 6 (hardening) where the actual limits get decided, not bolted on ad hoc now. Flagged prominently in both those phases below so it can't get lost.
- **No custom `error.tsx`/`loading.tsx` boundaries** — Next.js's generic fallbacks are used everywhere. Cosmetic, not launch-blocking; revisit in Phase 6's polish pass.
- **`.docx` export has no e2e coverage.** Low risk (it's a pure download, not stateful), but still a gap — added to Phase 3's backlog as a quick follow-up rather than reopening Phase 2 for one test.

None of these are severe enough to justify another Phase 2 cycle — they're either genuinely deferrable (rate limiting has a natural home once billing exists to pair with it) or low-risk (a download link). Re-opening Phase 2 for them would just delay Phase 3 without reducing real risk.
